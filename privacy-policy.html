CalcHub Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

CalcHub ("CalcHub", "we", "us", or "our") is a calculator application designed to provide calculation tools for scientific, financial, educational, and everyday use.

1. Information We Collect

CalcHub is designed to work without requiring users to create an account. Unless a feature explicitly states otherwise, we do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, passwords, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Calculator Data

Values entered into calculators are used to perform the requested calculations. Unless a feature specifically states otherwise, calculator inputs and results are processed locally on your device and are not intentionally sent to our servers.

3. Local Storage

CalcHub may use browser or device storage for features such as calculation history, preferences, or settings. This information may remain on your device until you clear the relevant application or browser data.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

The website or future versions of the app may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other technical functionality. If such services are introduced and collect information, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant practices.

5. Children's Privacy

CalcHub is a general-purpose calculator. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with CalcHub. However, no electronic service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when CalcHub's features or services change. The date shown above indicates when the policy was last revised.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please use the official CalcHub support or contact channel.
